How your overall percentage is calculated

Homework-01 and Homework-02: each mark ÷ 50 × 5. Quiz-01: raw mark ÷ 30 × 10. Quiz-02: raw mark ÷ 30 × 5. Each contribution is rounded to one decimal place, as in the gradebook. Quiz bonus marks are displayed separately and excluded from weighted contributions. An instructor-confirmed Quiz-01 weighted mark takes precedence when shown. The midterm is marked out of 30 and contributes up to 30 course percentage points.

Both sections: (Homework-01 + Homework-02 + Quiz-01 + Quiz-02 + midterm contributions) ÷ 55 × 100. Homework-03 (10%) and Final (35%) will count when their marks are released.

An overall result below 60% includes an invitation to seek additional support. The comparison uses the unrounded overall result. Missing marks for a released assessment keep the overall result pending; marks are not counted as zero.
