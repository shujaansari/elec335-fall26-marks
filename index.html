How your overall percentage is calculated

Homework-01: mark ÷ 50 × 5. Quiz-01: mark ÷ 30 × 10. Each contribution is rounded to one decimal place, as in the gradebook. An instructor-confirmed weighted mark takes precedence when shown. The midterm is marked out of 30 and contributes up to 30 course percentage points.

Overall percentage to date = (Homework-01 contribution + Quiz-01 contribution + midterm contribution) ÷ 45 × 100.

An overall result below 60% includes an invitation to seek additional support. The comparison uses the unrounded overall result. If a mark is pending, the overall result and the 60% comparison are held until all three marks are available.
